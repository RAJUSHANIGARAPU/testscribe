Frequently asked questions

How is TestScribe different from ChatGPT? ⌃
TestScribe is purpose-built for QA: it knows QA best practices, always covers edge cases and security scenarios, and outputs in formats your test tools expect. ChatGPT gives you generic output that you then need to reformat and review for coverage. TestScribe gets you from story to Cucumber-ready Gherkin in one click.
Can I cancel anytime? ⌃
Yes. Cancel at any time from your dashboard. You keep access until the end of your billing period. No questions asked, no cancellation fees.
Do you store my requirements or code? ⌃
Your inputs are sent to Claude AI for generation and stored in your account history for replay. We never train on your data. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. You can delete your history at any time from the dashboard.
What formats can I export? ⌃
Gherkin BDD (.feature), markdown table, and pytest stub files. Copy-paste or download. Solo and Pro plans also support CSV export of test case tables.
Is there a team plan? ⌃
Yes — coming soon. Email us at team@testscribe.ai for early access. Team plans will include shared workspaces, member management, and bulk generation.
